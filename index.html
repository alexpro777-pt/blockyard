Items

Right-click to use an item. The map, compass and glider work just by holding them. Flint & Steel starts fires: they spread through wood, leaves and wool, and water puts them out.

Portals: build an obsidian frame at least 4 wide and 5 tall (the corners can be left out), then light the inside with Flint & Steel. Stand in the portal for two seconds to travel to the Ember Realm. Each block there counts as 8 back home.

Beat the game: in the Ember Realm the compass points to shrines that hold Ember Cores. Collect 3 to unlock the Star Key, then use it to reach the Sky Arena. Break the 4 rune stones on the pillars (arrows work well) to drop the Stone Colossus's shield, then defeat it.

Armor

Click a piece to put it on or take it off. Armor soaks up part of every hit. Press V to see yourself.

Spark circuits

Right-click a lever or button to use it. Lay spark dust from a power source to a lamp, piston or blast block. A spark torch turns off when the block under it gets power, so you can build gates and clocks. Hold Shift to place a block against a lever instead of flipping it.
